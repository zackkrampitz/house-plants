House Plants / 09

ZZ Plant ‘Midori’

Your quick care reference

Water when

The potting mix is essentially dry throughout.

Its rhizomes store water. Check deeper soil and pot weight; low light can mean long gaps between watering.

How to water

Water slowly over the whole soil surface until water drains from the bottom. Let it drain fully, then empty the saucer or decorative outer pot.

Fertilizer

Balanced liquid houseplant fertilizer at half the label’s regular dilution every 6–8 weeks during active spring and summer growth. Pause during slow winter growth.

Support / moss pole

None normally needed. The plant supports itself.

Repot / upsize when

  • Thick underground rhizomes press against or distort the pot.
  • A root check shows a densely packed root mass with little loose soil.
  • The root mass makes thorough watering difficult.

Next pot: Choose a pot 1–2 inches (2.5–5 cm) wider, with drainage holes. Spring is a good time. Confirm crowded roots before upsizing; a few roots through a drainage hole alone are not enough.

Check the plant, not the calendar.
Watering changes with light, temperature, pot size and mix. Fertilize when actively growing; follow product limits.
