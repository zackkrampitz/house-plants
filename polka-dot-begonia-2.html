House Plants / 03

Polka Dot Begonia — Plant 2

Your quick care reference

Water when

The top 1–2 inches of soil feel dry.

Check 2.5–5 cm deep. Avoid constantly wet soil or prolonged drying of the whole root ball.

Light

Bright indirect light; gentle morning sun is fine. Avoid harsh midday sun.

How to water

Water slowly over the whole soil surface until water drains from the bottom. Let it drain fully, then empty the saucer or decorative outer pot. Direct water onto the soil rather than routinely wetting the leaves.

Fertilizer

Balanced liquid houseplant fertilizer at half the label’s regular dilution every 2–4 weeks during active spring and summer growth. Pause when winter growth slows.

Support / moss pole

Use a bamboo stake and loose ties if tall canes lean. A moss pole is unnecessary.

Repot / upsize when ↗

  • A root check shows a dense mass of circling roots with little loose soil.
  • The soil dries much faster after a thorough watering.
  • Crowded roots make watering difficult.

Next pot: Choose a pot 1–2 inches (2.5–5 cm) wider, with drainage holes. Spring is a good time. Confirm crowded roots before upsizing; a few roots through a drainage hole alone are not enough.

Propagation ↗

Stem cuttings with nodes. Tap for timing, steps and aftercare.

Check the plant, not the calendar.
Watering changes with light, temperature, pot size and mix. Fertilize when actively growing; follow product limits.
