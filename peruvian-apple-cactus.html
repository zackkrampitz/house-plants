Peruvian Apple Cactus

Your quick care reference

Water when

The soil is dry throughout the pot.

Check deep soil and pot weight. Water much less often during cool, low-light winter conditions.

How to water

Water slowly over the whole soil surface until water drains from the bottom. Let it drain fully, then empty the saucer or decorative outer pot.

Fertilizer

Cactus/succulent fertilizer at half the label’s regular dilution every 6–8 weeks during active spring and summer growth. Do not feed during winter dormancy.

Support / moss pole

No moss pole. Use a temporary stake only if the cactus is physically unstable; also assess light and pot stability.

Repot / upsize when

  • Roots densely fill the container with little loose soil.
  • The crowded root mass makes watering difficult.

Next pot: Choose a slightly wider pot with drainage and a free-draining cactus mix. A heavy, stable container helps a tall cactus; do not add excessive soil volume just to make it heavier.

Check the plant, not the calendar.
Watering changes with light, temperature, pot size and mix. Fertilize when actively growing; follow product limits.
