Money Tree

Your quick care reference

Water when

The top 2–3 inches of soil feel dry.

Check 5–7.5 cm deep. Avoid leaving the lower root zone constantly wet.

How to water

Water slowly over the whole soil surface until water drains from the bottom. Let it drain fully, then empty the saucer or decorative outer pot.

Fertilizer

Balanced liquid houseplant fertilizer at half the label’s regular dilution, monthly during active spring and summer growth. Pause when winter growth slows.

Support / moss pole

No moss pole. A temporary stake is useful only if the trunk is unstable.

Repot / upsize when ↗

  • A root check shows a dense mass of circling roots with little loose soil.
  • The soil dries much faster after a thorough watering.
  • Crowded roots make watering difficult.

Next pot: Choose a pot 1–2 inches (2.5–5 cm) wider, with drainage holes. Spring is a good time. Confirm crowded roots before upsizing; a few roots through a drainage hole alone are not enough.

Propagation ↗

Stem cuttings; air layering for larger branches. Tap for timing, steps and aftercare.

Check the plant, not the calendar.
Watering changes with light, temperature, pot size and mix. Fertilize when actively growing; follow product limits.
