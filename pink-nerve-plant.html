Pink Nerve Plant

Your quick care reference

Water when

The top ½ inch of soil starts to dry.

Check the plant and growing medium rather than following a fixed weekly schedule.

How to water

Water slowly across the soil until it drains, then empty the outer pot. Keep the deeper mix lightly moist, not saturated. Do not routinely wait for collapse; fittonia wilts quickly when too dry.

Fertilizer

Balanced liquid foliage fertilizer at quarter-to-half the label’s regular dilution about monthly during active growth. Reduce or pause feeding during slow winter growth.

Support / moss pole

None normally required. Use a stable pot; do not install a moss pole.

Repot / upsize when ↗

Repot when rooted stems and roots crowd the pot or the mix dries much faster despite thorough watering. Dense foliage alone does not prove it needs a larger pot.

Propagation ↗

Stem-tip cuttings with nodes. Tap for timing, steps and aftercare.

Light

Medium to bright indirect light. Keep out of harsh direct sun.
